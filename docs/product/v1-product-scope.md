# AIHQ v1 Product Scope

## V1 launch lane

AIHQ v1 focuses on one clear product path:

1. Approve Business Info.
2. Connect a live customer channel.
3. Receive customer conversations in Inbox.
4. Let an operator reply manually or enable AI Autopilot when runtime readiness allows it.
5. Track leads, customers, and basic performance reports from real backend data.

## V1-ready channels

The v1 launch channel set is:

- Website Chat
- Instagram
- Telegram

These channels must appear consistently in Home, Channels, Inbox readiness, and launch posture summaries.

## Phase 2 channels

The following channels are roadmap only and should not be marketed as launch-ready self-serve flows:

- WhatsApp
- Gmail
- Voice

## V1 product promise

AIHQ is not yet an all-in-one automation suite. The current promise should be:

AIHQ turns approved business information into a governed AI runtime and connects Website, Instagram, and Telegram conversations into one operator inbox.

## Do not promise yet

Avoid promising the following until the UI, backend evidence, and operational proof are complete:

- WhatsApp self-serve onboarding
- Gmail inbox integration
- Voice automation
- Content publishing
- Comment automation
- Workflow executions
- Incidents/ops automation
- Full CRM replacement

## Launch readiness checklist

Before calling the product launch-ready:

- Business Info approval works end to end.
- Website Chat can be configured, verified, previewed, and tested.
- Instagram connection, selection, disconnect, and reconnect flows work.
- Telegram bot connect, webhook readiness, disconnect, and inbox delivery work.
- Inbox manual reply works.
- AI Autopilot can be globally disabled/enabled.
- Conversation-level handoff works.
- Leads and Customers show real backend-created records.
- Reports show real data or honest empty states.
- Settings save AI policy to backend.
- Team invite/edit/disable works for owner/admin.
