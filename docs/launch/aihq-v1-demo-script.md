# AI HQ v1 Demo Script

## Demo goal

Show AI HQ as a focused launch system for customer conversations:

1. The business defines approved truth.
2. One customer channel is connected.
3. Conversations are operated from Inbox with safe AI support.

The demo should stay narrow. Do not present AI HQ v1 as a full CRM, voice platform, or complete automation OS.

## Opening line

AI HQ gives a business one controlled place to prepare its approved business truth, connect a live customer channel, and operate conversations from an inbox without letting AI answer from unapproved information.

## Demo flow

### 1. Sign in

Open the production AI HQ login page.

Say:

This is the operator workspace. We sign in with a workspace account, not a public chatbot account.

Expected screen:

- Email and password login
- No dead social login buttons
- No unimplemented recovery or public legal links in the visible launch entry

### 2. Home

Open Home.

Say:

Home shows the launch path. The product is intentionally strict: business truth first, one live channel second, inbox operation third.

Expected screen:

- Business truth status
- Channel status
- Inbox status
- Primary action points to the next real step

### 3. Setup assistant

Open the setup assistant from Home.

Say:

Setup is not a casual chatbot. It prepares a reviewable business draft. The draft must be approved before it becomes live runtime truth.

Expected screen:

- Setup entry opens from Home
- Copy stays in English
- No mixed Azerbaijani/English launch copy
- No unrelated old setup studio surfaces

### 4. Business Truth

Open Business Truth.

Say:

This is the approved source of business behavior. AI can only operate from approved truth. If truth or runtime is missing, the system stays blocked instead of guessing.

Expected screen:

- Current approved truth is visible
- Runtime/readiness state is visible
- No self-linking CTA back to the same Truth page
- No fake ready state when runtime is blocked

### 5. Channels

Open Channels.

Say:

AI HQ v1 only needs one live channel for launch. The channel must be connected and delivery-ready before Home can treat the workspace as launch-ready.

Expected screen:

- Website chat, Instagram, and Telegram are understandable
- Connected does not automatically mean delivery-ready
- Blocked channels explain what is missing
- No unsupported channels are presented as production-ready

### 6. Inbox

Open Inbox.

Say:

Inbox is where the operator sees customer conversations. AI support is safe because it is gated by approved truth and channel readiness.

Expected screen:

- Conversation list opens
- Lead/context information stays in place
- No CTA sends the user to frozen Leads
- Composer copy is English and launch-facing
- Unavailable states do not pretend there are zero messages

## Closing line

AI HQ is not trying to automate everything on day one. It first makes customer conversations safe: approved truth, one connected channel, and one inbox for operation.

## What not to say in the demo

Do not say:

- This is a full CRM.
- This replaces every business workflow.
- Voice is part of the v1 launch.
- Leads, comments, executions, or proposals are production launch surfaces.
- AI can answer freely without approved business truth.

## Demo success criteria

A successful demo proves:

- The app is production-accessible.
- The launch path is clear.
- AI safety depends on approved truth.
- One channel can be used as the live lane.
- Inbox is the operational surface.
- The product does not overpromise frozen features.
